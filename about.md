# About

*The Lantern Room* is a serialized mystery told in 100 daily chapters. Detective Elena Vega investigates the death of Dr. Vivian Mercer, a celebrated therapist found behind a locked door in Hartwell Bay — a case that reopens a wound from Elena's own past.

New chapters are added as they're written.
